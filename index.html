9 Machine Learning · Financial Technology

Stock Price Predictor

Predicts a stock's next-day close from its previous 100 trading days. I rebuilt the evaluation to test on unseen data against a baseline of tomorrow's close equaling today's.

  • Added a chronological split, training-only scaling, and automated leakage tests
  • Over 740 unseen GOOG trading days, the selected ridge model scored MAE $3.19 and RMSE $4.86 vs. the baseline's $3.18 and $4.84: no measurable edge
  • The original price-level forest scored MAE $83.34 because trees cannot predict above training prices

Not evidence of forecasting or trading skill.
